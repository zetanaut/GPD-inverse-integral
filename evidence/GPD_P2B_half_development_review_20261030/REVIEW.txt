P2B HALF-NOISE DEVELOPMENT: INDEPENDENT LOCAL REVIEW
30 October 2026

Decision

Accept the returned run as a completed, numerically eligible execution of the
frozen development case. Independently confirm STOP_DEVELOPMENT_FAILED: the
selected stage 60 improves observed-slice H+ and target A, but target B worsens
far beyond the permitted numerical tolerance. Independent assessment remains
unreleased. This is a scientific gate failure, not an execution failure.

The clean-input P2B preservation success remains valid. This noisy comparison
shows useful forward-boundary improvement and mixed off-forward effects. It
does not establish an overall validated improvement, an expected benefit over
new noise realizations, or calibrated experimental uncertainty. It also does
not establish that forward information can never help GPD inference.

What improves, and what does not

The added boundary information is accommodated substantially better than by
the reused baseline. On the 201-point forward support, channel relative L2
errors change as follows:
  Sigma: 46.79649% -> 8.31216%
  T:     31.66948% -> 7.10135%
  Hg:    34.05821% -> 8.16622%.
The integrated forward H+ relative L2 over [.02,.8] improves from 25.24383% to
7.01459%. Low/high-x tail diagnostics also improve on this known truth, but
those tails were not observed and these results do not supply uncertainty bounds.

The observed-slice aggregate improvement is concentrated in DGLAP:
  ERBL H+ relative L2:   17.64012% -> 19.48338% (worse)
  DGLAP H+ relative L2:  10.57101% ->  8.50877% (better).
Across the wider chart, both regions worsen:
  ERBL:   20.97103% -> 42.77176%
  DGLAP:  29.90692% -> 37.13806%.
The largest wider-chart slice error rises from 75.08065% to 93.15129%.
Observed-slice and wider-chart scores have distinct supports and must not be
presented as interchangeable estimates of one accuracy measure.

Wider-chart internal-channel effects are mixed:
  Sigma: 28.29546% -> 39.75807% (worse)
  T:    125.11851% -> 33.79804% (better)
  Hg:    80.79636% -> 48.59442% (better).
Thus better forward anchoring and some better internal channels do not imply
uniformly better off-forward charge-weighted reconstruction. The mechanism of
this tradeoff is not uniquely identified by the aggregate scores.

Recorded CFF matching RMS in original sigma units worsens from 0.435886 to
0.475308. Recorded ReH truth error at the input geometry improves modestly,
from 4.15346% to 3.80719% relative L2. These CFF diagnostics are
retained-source results, not an independent local NLO replay.

Disposition

Close this frozen P2B development comparison at STOP_DEVELOPMENT_FAILED and
retain every result. Independent assessment stays unreleased. Further scientific
work should diagnose the existing boundary/off-forward and target tradeoffs,
then specify a prospective estimator or information change with explicit
preservation and independent-assessment tests. The evidence supports improving
some information, not claiming uniformly improved reconstruction.

For the proposal note, retain the demonstrated clean-control result and describe
this noisy comparison as mixed benefit with a failed target safeguard. Its
29 October 'development pending' statement is now superseded by this review.
A defensible proposal can make resolving this tradeoff a research aim; it should
not claim that noisy forward augmentation is already validated.
