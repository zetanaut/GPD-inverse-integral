Decision

Accept the returned exact-center preservation result at its stated scope. The
normally selected, numerically eligible stage-60 output passes all four frozen
gates, and independent saved-network replay reproduces the reported field,
target and forward-boundary scores. This is a useful positive clean-input
control after the failed P2A preservation experiment.

It does not yet establish improved noisy reconstruction, calibrated uncertainty,
uniform field accuracy, or recovery of all internal channels. Scientifically,
the result supports proceeding to the already specified single half-noise
development comparison. That case is not launched or released by this review.
The omitted execution/acceleration evidence should be attached and reviewed
before completing its execution package; recover existing evidence rather
than repeat the completed exact-center fit.

What the positive result establishes

The unchanged flexible FiLM can accommodate the declared 36 correlated synthetic
forward samples together with the original CFF and finite moment conditions at
the selected state. The input covariance retains its finite ten-percent scale;
its formula, rank 36 and Cholesky whitening reproduce locally. The objective is
J_P1+chi2_PDF/160, with lambda_stab=0 and no new measured-moment injection.

At stage 60 the recorded components are J_CFF=2.382336749430799e-7,
J_poly=1.673021964481054e-7 and J_PDF=1.4248175669190178e-7, giving
J_joint=5.48017628083087e-7. The PDF term is independently replayed; the
other two terms and their sum are checked for record-level consistency only.
The CFF RMS in original sigma units is 0.000488091871416724.

The very small PDF whitened RMS is a residual to a noiseless central realization,
not an inferred uncertainty or evidence of calibrated sub-permille precision.
Even the original exact-CFF baseline has PDF RMS below 1. The new arm greatly
improves its central-boundary match, but the gate alone would not demonstrate
that PDF information is necessary or that it helps under random perturbations.

The result is substantially stronger than the earlier forward-only output-head
witness: it is a fresh full joint fit and an eligible selected state. Its scope
remains one Z1 truth, one initialization, exact CFFs and central synthetic PDF
values. It is not a real proton PDF application or independent noise assessment.

Tradeoffs that the four gates do not capture

The improvement is not uniform across the field or channel decomposition.
On the identical wider chart, the maximum slice H+ relative L2 increases from
1.75087% to 2.27701%. Wider-chart ERBL error increases slightly from 0.68165%
to 0.71635%, while DGLAP improves from 0.76313% to 0.49112%.

Wider-chart internal-channel errors all increase:
  Sigma:       3.72979% -> 7.30726%
  T_rescaled: 12.07882% -> 21.76231%
  Hg=xg:       0.62293% -> 1.32393%

At the forward boundary on the 201-point supported grid, their errors instead
fall to 0.05261%, 0.07397% and 0.00954%. Better boundary/channel matching does
not establish better off-forward channel identification. The charge-weighted
combination remains much better recovered than its individual quark components.

Post-selection tail checks improve too, but remain benchmark diagnostics. The
high-x [.8,1] forward H+ relative L2 is 0.62274%, versus 10.06368% for the
baseline; the high-x first-moment contribution error is -0.73124%. Finite-x
observations do not measure those tails or distinguish the program's known
same-forward D-term alternatives. No full-function uncertainty band follows.
