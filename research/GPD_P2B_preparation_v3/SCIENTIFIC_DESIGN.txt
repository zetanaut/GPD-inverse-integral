P2B v1: compatible synthetic forward information
26 October 2026

Purpose and present scope

Test whether uncertain forward-boundary information improves the existing Z1
reconstruction. The first comparison is CFF baseline versus CFF plus forward
information, with lambda_stab=0. P2A's failed penalty is not included by default.
This is a controlled information experiment, not an application of a real proton
PDF set. Original P2A results remain unchanged and its assessment stays stopped.

DESIGN.json specifies the prospective v1 choices before any new joint fit or
scientific noise draw. This package prepares the observation adapter and bounded
integration qualification. It does not contain a qualified full-fit/continuation
driver, and releases no joint fit. It defines a conditional two-fit pilot, not an
automatic campaign. User authorization in this turn is preparation for P2B.

Benchmark and physical conventions

Use the original Z1 structural generator for both the existing CFF targets and
the new forward information. The original source explicitly does not certify
individual-flavor PDFs, QCD positivity or proton sum rules. Do not call Z1 a
measured proton PDF or interpret the synthetic uncertainty as one.

Keep the original 3867-parameter FiLM, exported fresh seed31 vector, fixed-nf=3
NLO/MSbar maps and coupling, Q0^2=2 GeV^2 (Q0=sqrt(2) GeV), thirteen negative-t
moment slices, free permitted polynomial coefficients and numerical inequalities.
There is no PDF evolution, new kernel, fixed skewness/t profile, measured moment,
positivity restriction, or new experimental ImH/E/Htilde target.

At x>0 and xi=t=0, U=u+ubar, D=d+dbar, S=s+sbar give
  Sigma=U+D+S; T_rescaled=U-(D+S)/2; Hg=xg;
  H+=(2/9)(Sigma+T_rescaled)=(4U+D+S)/9.
The stored channels do not determine D and S separately. The xf conversion
helper divides LHAPDF-convention quark x*f values by x and retains xg. Algebraic
tests exercise that convention, but no LHAPDF provider or real set is selected
or imported. Any future provider needs an explicit scheme/flavor/coupling and
metadata adapter. No numerical type or metadata assertion alone certifies that
an external set has matching physics.

Finite boundary observation and covariance

Use twelve fixed geometric x locations from 0.02 through 0.8 inclusive, three
stored channels each, at xi=t=0 and Q0. Vector order is x-major/channel-minor.
These are 36 correlated synthetic boundary measurements. They are not a claim
to 36 independent measurements or to a continuous PDF band.

With f_ic the central Z1 boundary value, define once, independently of fitted
parameters,
  s_ic=0.10*max(abs(f_ic), 0.05*max_j abs(f_jc));
  Kx_ij=exp(-abs(log(x_i)-log(x_j))/0.7);
  Kc=[[1,.5,0],[.5,1,0],[0,0,1]];
  C_(ic,jd)=s_ic Kx_ij Kc_cd s_jd.

The ten-percent scale, five-percent floor, support, correlation length and channel
correlation are declared sensitivity-design choices, not fitted hyperparameters
or empirically calibrated PDF uncertainties. They were specified before inspecting
this package's boundary diagnostics. Their usefulness must be judged under this
scope; there is no precision sweep or retrospective retuning in v1.

The supplied covariance is full rank (36), condition number about 1.155e4. Use
Cholesky whitening of the complete vector. Reject singular/indefinite inputs;
do not invent jitter, zero uncertainty, diagonal errors or a pseudoinverse of a
future replica covariance. Unmeasured continuum directions have no penalty,
not zero uncertainty. Refining a display or checking grid does not add residuals.

CENTRAL_FORWARD.npz contains only x, observed boundary values and covariance.
The fitting adapter imports no truth generator. The central control sets observed
values equal to the generator but RETAINS this finite covariance. It is a noiseless
realization control, not an uncertainty-free equality boundary condition.

The later development draw is prospective: PCG64 seed 2026102601, 36 standard
normal variates in the declared vector order, y_PDF=f+L*z, L=chol(C). Archive the
actual z and y bytes and generator/version identities once after release; reuse
those arrays across platforms. Do not redraw for an unfavorable result. This
draw has not been generated. Independence from CFF perturbations is an explicit
synthetic assumption; it must not be transferred to real PDF/CFF data sets.

Objective and estimator

Append r_PDF=L^-1(F_theta(x,0,0)-y_PDF)/sqrt(160) to the 6010 original residuals.
There are 6046 residuals, 3867 parameters and the original numerical constraints.
Use the actual vector Jacobian, not one scalar sqrt(chi2_PDF) residual.

  J_joint=J_P1+chi2_PDF/160
         =(chi2_CFF+chi2_PDF)/160+J_poly.

Keep original CFF sigmas and row masses, including repeated coordinates. There
is no extra PDF strength or division by PDF grid count, and lambda_stab=0.
Because the reused half-amplitude CFF perturbation has the ORIGINAL sigmas in the
loss, this is a fixed-estimator sensitivity study with a declared working metric,
not the exactly specified likelihood of the half-amplitude generative law.

All scientific runs must use the exported fresh seed31 array, 400 Adam updates,
at most 60 original outer iterations and 12000 total solver evaluations including
continuations. Preserve deterministic objective/seed/stage ranking and original
output eligibility. Save 0/20/40/60 or actual terminal stage, named objective
terms, stopping/KKT diagnostics and actual resource costs. No truth-selected
checkpoint, fitted-state warm start, new architecture or true-moment injection.

Representation, boundary and tails

The quark network is odd and vanishes at x=0; all channels have (1-x^2)^2
envelopes. Restrict the observation to the declared finite support. t=0 is outside
the original negative-t collocation range. New boundary data can constrain the
network there without proving its finite-t interpolation or extrapolation.

The local forward-only diagnostic fixes hidden weights at fresh seed31 and solves
once for the existing output head on 81 training points. It checks 161 staggered
points with predeclared rank tolerance and accuracy limits. This is not a joint
fit or a proposed initializer. It demonstrates sampled boundary representability;
its large head norm and cancellation do not demonstrate easy or stable joint
optimization. Simultaneous CFF/moment feasibility is still a joint-fit question.

No tail, endpoint or full-moment observations are added. The separate analytic
Z1 tail diagnostic estimates 8.50% of its forward H+ first moment outside
[.02,.8]; this is scoring-only generator information, not an error bound for a
reconstructed field. Point observations also leave between-point freedom.

The previously constructed D-term alternatives share forward functions. This
new information gives them identical boundary likelihoods. It cannot resolve
that ambiguity or provide a full off-forward uncertainty band.

Conditional pilot and decisions

1. P2B_exact_center_s31: original exact CFF input plus central forward vector
   with finite covariance; reuse the P1 exact baseline. One new fit, conditional
   on preparation/integration review and a qualified execution driver.
   Require normal numerical eligibility, observed-slice H+ relative L2 <=.02,
   absolute target A/B relative errors <=.02, and PDF whitened RMS <=1.
   All tests use unrounded inclusive values. These are declared methodological
   safeguards, not confidence or QCD thresholds. Failure stops v1 expansion.

2. P2B_half_development_s31: original inspected half-amplitude CFF vector plus
   the one prospective coherent PDF draw; reuse the P1 half baseline. One new fit
   only after case 1 passes and is reviewed. Its existing CFF realization means
   this is development, not an independent assessment.
   Advance only if observed-slice squared H+ error improves beyond the frozen
   tolerance and neither target's absolute relative error worsens beyond its
   analogous tolerance: 1e-10+1e-6*max(abs(candidate),abs(baseline)). Keep every
   outcome. Do not add a strength/grid/covariance/seed after seeing this result.

Targets retain xi=.275, t=-.363, Q0: A=5 integral_.2^.4 H+ dx and
B=integral_0^1 x H+ dx. Score observed-slice, ERBL/DGLAP, wider-chart, internal
channels, A/B, CFF fit/truth errors, boundary fit/truth errors and omitted-support
behavior separately. No threshold or improvement on one support transfers to
another. Independent CFF/PDF noise pairs and another truth family are later
experiments with separately frozen designs; none is released here.

The eventual four-way baseline/stabilizer/PDF/combined comparison remains a
possible design. The failed P2A candidate is historical evidence, not a required
ingredient or an automatic combined arm. Real proton PDFs remain a separate
compatibility and benchmark problem.

Next concrete work

unchanged constraints, model construction, trial evaluation and checkpoint values
with at most one diagnostic outer step. It reports whether a nonempty active
